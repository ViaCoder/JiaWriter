# Business email and memos

## Structure

- The subject line names the matter and the action needed, for example "Please confirm: sample delivery on 5 November".
- The first paragraph states the purpose and the request.
- List the details by item with dates, quantities, owners and deadlines.
- Close with the next step and contact details; one courtesy line is enough.

## Style

- Match the greeting and tone to the relationship with the recipient.
- Keep paragraphs short, one matter each.
- Write dates in full and give amounts with their currency.

## Minutes

- Record time, place, attendees and agenda.
- For each item record the decision, the owner and the due date.

## Final checks

- Run style_check and check that names, dates and figures agree throughout.
