# Academic writing

Writes papers, literature reviews, proposals and abstracts: a clear question, traceable evidence, conclusions within their limits.

## Preparation

- Confirm the discipline, the journal or school format, the citation style (such as APA or GB/T 7714) and the length.
- Import the user's literature and record, for each source, its claims, methods, findings and page numbers.

## Structure

- The abstract states the question, method, main findings and significance.
- The introduction gives the background, the state of research and the question this paper answers.
- Develop the body as method, results, discussion, or by argument; each section opens with its task.
- The conclusion answers the question of the introduction and states limits and further work.

## Citations

- Cite a source for every claim and figure, in one consistent style.
- Cite only imported literature and public sources found by search, with author, year and page.
- Paraphrase faithfully; put direct quotations in quotation marks with the page.

## Style

- Use precise terms and define each at first use.
- Match the strength of each claim to its evidence and state the scope of each conclusion.
- Choose an academic export profile when exporting.
