# Narrative fiction

## Preparation

- Confirm the length, point of view, readers' age and genre.
- Write down each main character's goal, obstacle and change, and where the story starts and ends.

## Structure

- Open in a concrete scene and bring the character to the problem quickly.
- Raise the conflict step by step in the middle; every scene moves the plot or reveals a character.
- The ending answers the question of the opening and shows the change through action.

## Craft

- Show characters through action, dialogue and detail, and let readers draw their own conclusions.
- Give each character a voice that fits who they are; each line of dialogue moves something forward.
- Use concrete sensory detail and comparisons drawn from the character's own experience.
- Keep the timeline and the names of characters consistent.

## Final checks

- Run style_check with a negation threshold of 5; keep negations in dialogue where they fit the character.
