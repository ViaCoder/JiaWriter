# Parameters of reports

Medians (middle half in brackets) measured on real published reports in 2026: 131 survey and inspection reports, 193 research and statistical analyses. Read when the draft's sentences or wording feel off; a value outside the range is not wrong by itself. Length follows the user's request.

| Measure | Survey and inspection | Research and analysis |
|---|---|---|
| Average sentence length (characters) | 48 (44–53) | 53 (45–59) |
| 、 per 1,000 characters | 21 (16–25) | 16 (11–24) |
| 必须, 应当, 不得, 严禁 per 3,000 characters | 0 (0–1) | 0 (0–0) |
| 要, 切实, 确保 per 3,000 characters | 9 (6–15) | 6 (2–12) |
| 建议, 可考虑 per 3,000 characters | 0 (0–1) | 0 (0–0) |
| Reports that use percentages | 52% | 75% |

- Reports argue with facts, not with orders: mandatory words are almost absent, and requirement words belong to the measures.
- A report without real data argues its point instead of inventing figures.
