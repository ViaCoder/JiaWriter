# Statutory document samples (layout reference)

Seven policy documents published on the Chinese government website: a reply, a letter of reply, two notices, a decision and two regulations. Public documents, free to use.

Their value is what the structure template does not show: the document number (国函〔2026〕80号), the addressee (山东省人民政府：), the issuing body's signature, the date of issue and the publication mark （此件公开发布）. When writing a notice, request or reply, check the layout against the real document. The transitions, the order of a reply and the closings are in `公文语料/文章结构模板.md`.

| 通知 | 国办印发《关于国务院行政复议案件处理程序的若干规定》的通知 | 1,442 |
| 决定 | 关于修改和废止部分行政法规的决定 | 2,812 |
| 条例 | 集成电路布图设计保护条例 | 6,423 |
| 条例 | 关于出境入境管理的规定 | 3,370 |

- A notice that issues a plan (印发……的通知) is a notice; the plan is its attachment.
- Length follows the matter, from 508 to 6,927 characters; a short one is not divided into numbered parts.
- These are State Council documents: a city or county document is plainer and shorter, so do not copy their level of wording.
