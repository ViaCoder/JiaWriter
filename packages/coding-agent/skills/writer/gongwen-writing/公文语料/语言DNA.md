# Language of official documents

Habits of real official documents. Read for word-level detail.

## Sentences

- Reports average 48–53 characters a sentence, the length coming from parallel elements separated by 、 (16–21 per 1,000 characters; see `参数卡.md`).
- Short sentences are few, placed where the text must hit hardest; a text with none has no rise and fall.

## Tone

- The default strength word is 要 + verb.
- 必须, 应当, 不得, 严禁 are rare (reports use almost none); more and they lose force.
- Upward documents use 建议 for 要; downward documents keep 要; 希望 only at the end of a speech.
- 进一步 sparingly. One's own side is 我县, 我局 or 本机关, never 我 or 你.

## Figures

- Statistics, percentages, years and document numbers in Arabic numerals; set terms, approximations and ordinals in Chinese numerals (十八大, 第一书记).
- Policies are cited by their full title, rarely by document number.
- Data is not required: what cannot be verified is left out and the point is argued instead.
