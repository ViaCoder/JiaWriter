# Stance and attribution

Stance, quotation, data, attribution and measure in real official documents. Read when writing upward (research reports, requests, proposals).

## Stance

- Most open from the national strategy or the overall position, many from the policy basis or the mandate; only speeches to the public open from a scene on the ground.
- A flaw of the system is almost never the opening: to criticize a system, first state its strategic place and what it has achieved.
- How high to stand follows the direction of the document, its mandate, the body's level and the reader; a lower body does not set the tone for a higher one.

## Quotation

- Authorities in the order 总书记 → 党中央 → 国务院 → provincial or ministry leadership → law → the body's own leaders → experts, never reversed.
- Quote only what the materials hold in full, with its source; a quotation of the body's own leader gives name, post and words, to show the mandate.
- As many quotations as the argument needs; experience material about the body's own work needs no authority behind it.

## Data

- Data sits in the first half, in the situation and results; the measures use almost none.
- Data works best as a gap between two figures, as absolute number, relative number and rate together, as a comparison of town and country or inside and outside, or turned into what one person feels.
- When, where, how many and how the research was done: only when the materials say so; no invented visits or meetings.

## Cases

- Good practice with full names, place, project and amount, so others can follow it.
- Negative cases already dealt with in public: as the public reports have them, with 原 before the post.
- People and bodies anonymous in the materials stay anonymous; criticism of peers or superiors is general (一些地方, 有的部门).

## Attribution

- Achievements are credited to leadership and support, not to the body itself.
- Problems: objective causes go to history, geography, the stage of development and outside conditions, with no blame; subjective causes go to thinking and style, limited to 个别, 部分 or 少数.

## Measure

- 建议 only upward; a body that can decide does not suggest. 希望 for those one cannot order; 要 and 切实 within one's own system; 严禁 and 不得 only in normative documents such as opinions.
- A proposal names which body does what; the weakest asks only that a study begin, and promises nothing beyond one's authority.

## Sensitive points

- A disagreement between departments: both sides stated, put down to a difference of work, with no accusation.
- A specific criticism written as a general case; the heaviest judgment in the words of a public report or another person; one's own responsibility first, then demands on those below.

## Endings and what not to do

- An upward research report ends on its last proposal, with no 特此报告; modesty is said once, at the start.
- No opening with a declaration of attention, no empty verbs to fill space; a problem part needs a case or a figure, not a row of four-character phrases.
