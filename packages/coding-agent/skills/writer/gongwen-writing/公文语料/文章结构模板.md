# Structure by genre

The order and weight of the level-1 sections, and how a section is built, as read from real official documents. Read only the genre being written. Items within a section are numbered （一）（二） and open with their point; never 一是 or 一要.

## Research and survey reports (调研报告)

- Order: why and how the survey was done, from the materials only (a short opening ending with “现将有关情况报告如下”) → practices and results → problems and causes → measures. The three main parts weigh about the same; one part far heavier makes it experience material instead.
- Reporting variant: why → how the problems show → causes → measures (the heaviest); level-1 headings state the judgment itself.
- Within a part: a lead sentence gives the background and the judgment and says how many kinds follow → the items → cases or data as proof. A problem may be named in the words of those it affects when that says more than an abstract label.
- A transition opens the new part; it does not close the one before.

## Leader's speech (领导讲话)

- Order: address, purpose, agenda → why → what → who does it; three level-1 parts. Level-1 headings: an action, then its purpose (about 17 characters).
- Each task its own paragraph, opening with the task in one sentence, then how; questions and figures of speech may set the pace; it ends with a call and a pledge.

## Work opinion (工作意见) and work plan (工作方案)

- Opinion: basis and purpose (“按照……根据……结合……制定本意见”) → general requirements → key tasks (the bulk) → safeguards. Plan: basis → requirements → key tasks (the bulk) → steps → organization; an event plan: background → aim → time, place, people → form → publicity.
- Within a task: （一） a short verb-object heading with a full stop → one sentence on why or the overall path → 要 sentences with concrete actions, or progress by year or stage. No transitions between parts. Level-1 headings have 4–8 characters.

## Experience material (经验材料) and summary (经验总结)

- Material: background → practices (the bulk) → lessons that turn the case into a rule others can use; a lead sentence names the axis the subheadings follow; nothing on the unit's unsolved problems.
- Summary: overall results → three stages of about equal weight, with no problems or lessons part; each practice ends on an absolute number, not a percentage.

## Statutory documents (通知, 请示, 批复, 函)

- Layout as in `参考/法定公文样例/`. The transition fixes the genre: a notice “现将有关事项通知如下：”, a reply “……收悉。现批复如下：”. A reply states its position first, the reasons after; length follows the matter. Level headings may end with a colon.
- Closings: 通知 “特此通知”, 请示 “妥否，请批示”, 报告 “特此报告”, 函 “特此函复”.

## Across genres

- Plans, opinions and experience materials put their weight on what is done; reports, speeches and summaries weigh their parts about equally.
- The more binding a document, the fewer its transitions; the more it must persuade, the clearer they are.
